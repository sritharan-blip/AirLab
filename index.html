AirLab controls

  1. Enable your camera.

    Allow camera access, sit in good light, and keep both hands visible. Select your built-in or USB webcam under Camera source. Auto prefers a computer webcam over named phone or virtual feeds. Video is processed on your device.

  2. Point, then pinch.

    Your index finger steers the cursor. Bring your thumb and index finger together over an object to grab it.

  3. Move and release.

    Lift and move the object. Separate your fingers to drop it, or move quickly as you release to throw.

  4. Control the whole lab.

    Pinch over buttons to select them. Pinch and drag the bounce slider. Open your fingers between clicks. Each hand can hold its own object. Choose 4 hands for group mode. With Magnet or Repel selected, pinch and hold over the scene to apply a force field. Hold an open cursor near the top or bottom edge to scroll. Stretch: pinch the same object with two hands, then spread or twist. With a mouse, Shift-drag an object in Stretch mode. Draw: pinch and trace a line, then release to create a solid platform. Portal: click or pinch two spots; released, unlinked objects teleport through the rings. Rope or Spring: select an object and then another object or an empty anchor point. A mouse or touchscreen works too.

Keyboard: Space pauses, R resets, F toggles focus mode. Freeze toggles an object’s fixed position; Clone copies an object; Erase removes it and its connections. Save locally stores one complete scene on this device, and Export JSON makes a portable scene file. Performance Auto adapts drawing detail; choose Fast on slower computers. This is a visual 2D physics simulation; it provides no physical force feedback. Objects use simulated units. Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost.
